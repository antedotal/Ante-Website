## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Sleek, high-energy modern app launch video tailored for university students battling procrastination.
- Interpretation: Confident pacing, silky transitions, crisp contrast, premium typography, zero fluff.
- Angle: University students don't need another generic to-do list they can ignore. Ante gives them real stakes: monetary loss and unforgiving friend verifiers.
- Hook: "Stop procrastinating. Stop scrolling."
- Outro / punchline: "Ante. Stop procrastinating. Start doing."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Flashy low-contrast text that compromises readability

## Visual Identity
- Background: Deep teal/slate gradient `#001A20` → `#003A4A`, accented by `#236597` and `#4A8B9F`, with crisp off-white `#FAFBFC` card panels.
- Text: Crisp White `#FFFFFF`, High-contrast Charcoal `#1A1A1A`, Muted `#94A3B8`.
- Accent: Electric Cyan `#00A4C6`, Soft Cyan `#A8E8F8`, Brand Blue `#4A8B9F`.
- Display font: `Google Sans Flex`, `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.
- Body font: `Google Sans Flex`, `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.
- Visual references from the project: App phone mockups with curved borders, sleek shadows, subtle glassmorphism cards, cyan glowing borders.
