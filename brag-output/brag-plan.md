# Brag Plan: Ante

## What is this app?
Ante is the social task manager designed to destroy procrastination by combining social accountability from friends with real financial stakes on your daily goals.

## The angle
University students don't need another pretty to-do list app that they can snooze or ignore. They need skin in the game. With Ante, you stake real cash, designate a trusted friend to verify proof before your deadline, and either get verified or pay up. "Your friends aren't forgiving. Neither is your wallet."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-energy, relatable hook addressing every university student: "Stop procrastinating. Stop scrolling." Bold typography transitions against the signature glowing teal background, asking the question every student avoids: "You suck at keeping yourself accountable."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Setting the Ante ($10 on the line):** Showing the actual Ante payment hold interface where real stakes turn abstract intentions into urgent priorities.
- **Enlisting Friends as Verifiers:** Showing the friend verification workflow ("Invite your friends to keep you honest and call out the excuses").
- **Proof Submission or Pay Up:** Highlighting the verification submission step ("Upload photo proof. Get verified — or pay up").

## Outro / punchline
"Ante. Stop procrastinating. Start doing." Clean, confident logo landing with the waitlist call to action and App Store / Android badges.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Set Task & Stake ($10):** "Finish assignment draft" with a $10 ante on the line.
2. **Appoint Verifier:** Friend selected as judge to review submitted photo proof.
3. **Verdict:** Task verified — cash saved, goal conquered. (Or pay for your laziness, literally).

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook (The University Struggle) — 3.5s
- **Visual:** Deep glowing teal background. Large typography dynamically cycles: "Stop Procrastinating." → "Stop Scrolling." A sleek phone mockup slides in displaying overdue tasks ("Finish essay draft", "Study for midterms").
- **Sequential/interaction:** Cycling headline words smoothly transition; phone mockup glides into focus from right.
- **Audio intent:** Energetic groove kicks in, setting a modern, engaging pace.
- **Audio-coupled idea:** Beat-aligned headline word change at 1.5s.
- **Music:** Upbeat, clean electronic bed.
- **Transition mood:** Clean slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Ante (Put Money On The Line) — 5.0s
- **Visual:** Left column highlights the core mechanic: "Put real money on the line." Subtitle: "Pay for your laziness, literally." Right side showcases the interactive Ante selector (`payment_hold_mockup.png`) locking in a $10 stake.
- **Sequential/interaction:** Stake selector taps $10, glowing cyan badge activates with confirmation.
- **Audio intent:** Tactile feedback emphasizing real commitment.
- **Audio-coupled idea:** Card slide-in (`casino/card-slide-1.ogg`) at 3.6s, chip stake click (`casino/chip-lay-1.ogg`) at 5.0s.
- **Transition mood:** Soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Accountability (Friends Verify Proof) — 5.5s
- **Visual:** Split showcase of the social accountability loop: "Add your friends as verifiers." Phone mockup shows friend selector (`add-friend-mockup-frame.png`), followed by the proof submission screen (`task_verification_mockup.png`): "Upload a photo or receipt. If they approve, you keep your cash."
- **Sequential/interaction:** Friend card selected with green verified badge; proof photo animates into review slot.
- **Audio intent:** Trust and social stakes.
- **Audio-coupled idea:** UI selection tap (`interface/click_001.ogg`) at 9.0s; success confirmation sound (`casino/chips-collide-1.ogg`) at 12.5s.
- **Transition mood:** Dramatic sweep → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Payoff & Outro (Ante) — 4.5s
- **Visual:** Cinematic centered logo reveal with the vibrant Ante icon, bold title "Ante", tagline "Stop procrastinating. Start doing.", and call-to-action "Join the Waitlist — Built for uni students".
- **Sequential/interaction:** Ante icon scales and settles with soft cyan radial bloom; title and CTA pills fade in crisply.
- **Audio intent:** Satisfying resolution, memorable sonic branding.
- **Audio-coupled idea:** Resonant bell hit (`impact/impactBell_heavy_000.ogg`) locked to 17.02s strong beat.
- **Music:** Smooth fade-out to silence at 18.5s.

**Music mood for this video:** Upbeat, modern, polished productivity energy.
**Audio summary:** Starts with an energetic groove driving relatable student pain points, builds tactile momentum through stake setting and friend verification, and resolves in a crisp, confident brand payoff.
