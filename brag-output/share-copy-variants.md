Most to-do lists fail because there's zero penalty for ignoring them.

Ante is a social task manager built on two psychological levers that actually work: social pressure and monetary deterrence. You set a task, stake real cash ($5 to $50), and assign a friend to verify your proof.

If they approve, you keep your cash. If not, you pay for your laziness.
